## Summary

- codex-T4-d1: partly confirmed. The 31 methods lack `# Errors`, but
  their failure conditions are documented in each type's `# Serialization`
  section. It duplicates the policy question already attached to
  codex-T4-6, and the "handwritten only" scope omits derived validating
  deserializers.
- codex-D2-d1: partly confirmed. The facts hold, but it is a one-hop
  addendum to the already-confirmed `ParameterSnapshot`/`EditToken`
  omissions and falls under the D2-4 closure policy question. Its severity
  is lower than stated, since no consumer names the type.
- codex-C4-v3-1: confirmed. Low at most. Documenting the exception is the
  feasible fix, since "preserve the minimum" cannot hold in saturated `f32`
  coordinates. It is distinct from claude-C4-v3-2.
- Extensions in codex-delta3: all confirmed. The Constraints and
  text-length items overlap my new findings claude-N3-v3-2 and
  claude-N3-v3-1 (filing dispute only). CHANGES.md still says 41 numeric
  rows instead of 31.
