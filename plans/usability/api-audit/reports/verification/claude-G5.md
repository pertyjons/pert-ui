## My findings disputed

Codex records no finding for several names my report includes, or classifies them differently. I re-checked each one.

1. **claude-D2-4 (inspection types in `ComponentDetails`; `DragPreview`, `DRAG_THRESHOLD`):** codex lists `Semantics`, `SemanticRole`, `SemanticValue`, `SemanticAction` and `InspectValue` as "inspection … no membership finding", and `DragPreview`/`DRAG_THRESHOLD` as helpers. **Maintained.** The prelude guarantees three times that a custom component needs no other import: `R/lib.rs:324-326`, `F/lib.rs:53-55` and root.md:39-40. The prelude's `ComponentDetails`, returned by `Component::inspect`, has public fields `Semantics`, `ValidationHints`, `BoxModelInspection` and `InspectValue` (`R/diagnostics.rs:646-658`). `EventContext::start_drag(payload, permitted, preview: DragPreview)` (`R/runtime/drag.rs:132-137`) requires `DragPreview`. Codex's reading ("inspection stays at the root") is one side of the conflict D2-4 records. Codex does not address the guarantee sentence. The finding stays a project-rule conflict with a policy question on the closure.
2. **claude-D2-7 (`NativeWindowId`, `AppShutdownError` wording):** codex classes `NativeWindowId` as "host operations … plumbing" and says all five native additions satisfy the facade rule. **Maintained, with stronger evidence.** `App::on_close` reports `&NativeWindowId` (`F/app.rs:465-469`), and the rule includes "`App` and the types it reports" (`F/lib.rs:50-51`). It is also returned by the prelude's `ChildInfo::detached_window` (`R/runtime/native_windows.rs:1417`) and `EventContext::native_window` (`:1289`), both one hop from `Component` signatures. On `AppShutdownError`, codex is right that its membership fits the rule, because it is the payload of `AppError::Shutdown` (`F/app.rs:75`). My point is narrower and still holds: the facade's error sentence names only `AppError` (`F/lib.rs:58-60`).
3. **claude-D2-1, `HeldInputId`/`HeldCancellation` (medium):** codex classes `HeldInputId` as host plumbing. **Maintained at medium confidence, as a classification question.** `Runtime::register_held_input(owner, keys, modifiers: Modifiers, action, cancel: FnMut(KeyCode, HeldCancellation)) -> HeldInputId` (`R/runtime/held.rs:71-83`) is a keyboard registration made by an application (diagram_editor app.rs:198). A `Runtime` method alone does not make a type a member, because `Runtime` also names capture and inspection types, so whether this counts as "keyboard types an application configures" is for the maintainer.
4. **claude-D2-5, host-delivery family:** codex records no finding for `DeliveryMonitor`, `DeliverySnapshot`, `SinkError` and `SinkErrorKind`, and lists `EditToken` and `ParameterValue` as missing members. **Corrected in part.** `EditToken` (returned by the prelude `EditSession::token`, `R/parameter_binding.rs:730`) and `ParameterValue` (the bound of `Parameter<T>`, `:283`) are binding-side, not host-side. I withdraw "unclassified" for them and accept codex's classification: rule-included. `DeliveryStatus` moves out of the host family too (delta finding d2). `DeliveryMonitor`, `DeliverySnapshot`, `TerminalEvent`, `PreviewEvent`, `SinkError`, `SinkErrorKind` and `DeliveryResolution`, which `ParameterHost`'s sink and recovery methods name, remain a policy question ("hosts" against "host-integration", `R/lib.rs:304-305` against `:320-322`). Codex's no-finding is one answer to that question, not a fact against it.
5. **claude-D2-2 is incomplete:** see the `ListDropMark` row above. I add `ListDropMark` to D2-2.

### claude-D2-d1 The facade prelude's rustdoc gates its native members on the wrong feature

- **Item and kind:** D2, project rule (the prelude's own membership rustdoc, checked with feature conditions).
- **Paths and configurations:** `pert_ui::prelude::{App, AppError, AppShutdownError, ErrorAction, NativeCloseAction}`. The listing places them in the prelude in default, cpu, serde, images, image-formats, svg and no-default-desktop-cpu (`types.txt` prelude column).
- **Observation (signature):** `F/lib.rs:50-51` says "With the `desktop` feature it also includes `App` and the types it reports", and `:59` says "alongside `AppError` with `desktop`". The re-export is gated on `native` (`F/lib.rs:69-71`). `desktop-cpu = ["native", "cpu"]` enables `native` without `desktop` (crates/pert-ui/Cargo.toml `[features]`), and in that configuration the version 2 column shows these names in the prelude.
- **Rule:** the prelude rustdoc's membership statement must match its members; rubric D2 says "with feature conditions".
- **Severity:** low. A desktop-cpu user gets more than the text promises, not less. **Confidence:** high.
- **Suggested fix:** say "with `native` (enabled by `desktop` and `desktop-cpu`)" in both sentences. This can be combined with D2-7's wording fix. Not breaking.
- **Destination:** app-author slice 9, with D2-7.

### claude-D2-d2 Binding status values the prelude's `Parameter` returns are missing

- **Item and kind:** D2, project rule.
- **Paths and configurations:** `pert_ui_runtime::{DeliveryStatus, AuthorityStatus}`, in all configurations. `Revision` belongs here too, but it is already confirmed above under codex-D2-1.
- **Observation (signature):**
  - `Parameter::delivery_status(&self) -> ReadSignal<DeliveryStatus>` (`R/parameter_binding.rs:327`). `DeliveryStatus` is documented as "Read-only delivery health of one parameter, shared by built-in and custom controls" (`R/parameter_binding/delivery.rs:169`).
  - `Parameter::authority_status(&self) -> Option<ReadSignal<AuthorityStatus<T>>>` (`R/parameter_binding/remote.rs:686`): "Custom controls can reproduce this policy with an update-phase binding read" (`:684`).
  - Both are read through the prelude binding, not through `ParameterHost`. My report filed `DeliveryStatus` under the unclassified host family, which was wrong. `AuthorityStatus` appears in neither report.
- **Rule:** `R/lib.rs:304-305` "state and parameters: … bindings and hosts"; `:324-326` "a custom component can be written from `use pert_ui::prelude::*` alone".
- **Severity:** low. **Confidence:** high for `DeliveryStatus`. Medium for `AuthorityStatus`, because its producers `AuthorityReceipt` and `RemoteHost` are host integration.
- **Suggested fix:** add both, or say in the rule that remote-authority status stays with the host types. Additive.
- **Destination:** app-author slice 9, with D2-5.
