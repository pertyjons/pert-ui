## My findings disputed

- **N2 disposition — correct in part.** Withdraw “no additional findings” for the reactive-read, capability/restriction, role-table accessor and typed-downcast proposals verified above. Also accept the edit-session and selection-notification naming proposals in claude-N2-5. These remain proposals, not newly established project rules. Maintain the distinction between search exhaustion and terminal progress, and between retained initial names and writable application-owned names, using the evidence in those rows.

- **N5 disposition — maintain.** Neither dropped family prefixes nor abbreviated family names demonstrate a word-order departure. Claude’s two suggestions can proceed as policy/discovery proposals; `R/list_drag.rs:47` and `R/quick_pick.rs:281` do not refute my qualified N5 “no findings.”

- **codex-B4-1, `FormStatus` omission — correct.** Include it in the naming-scope discussion. It is active coordination with a focus operation (`R/field_status.rs:173`, `:284`), rather than an owned status value. Whether that makes it a controller under the rule remains a maintainer decision.

- **codex-B4-1, `EditorSession` exclusion — maintain as a policy question.** It is cloneable, UI-thread-only and drives attached edit sessions (`R/editor_session.rs:119`, `:152`, `:173`, `:181`). The editor-session contract specifies that protocol (`docs/contracts.md:931`), but gives no explicit naming exception. Claude’s protocol distinction is valid evidence for adopting an exception, not proof that one already exists.

- **codex-B4-2, captured-data scope — qualify.** My distinction between ordinary reports/geometry values and captured state was too implicit. `WindowPlacement` and `ValidationReport` demonstrably capture presentation observations (`R/windows.rs:20`, `R/validation.rs:208`). Include them in the broad-rule policy discussion. Operation reports remain distinguishable; the maintainer must decide whether the naming rule covers them.

- **codex-B4-4, `Pending<T>` — maintain the observation and policy question.** Claude classifies it as a handle rather than a receipt. It nevertheless reports one queued request, with one-time reply consumption across threads (`R/inspection.rs:556`, `:588`, `:610`, `:640`). Its ownership differs materially from UI receipts, supporting an explicit exception rather than adding a UI-local binding.

- **codex-B4-5, operation outcomes — maintain with clarification.** The verified operation results support the broad-vocabulary proposal. Claude correctly identifies the inverse problem under a narrow reading of “interaction.” My use of `PreviewOutcome` as a comparator does not establish that the current contract already permits every operation-result use (`C/edit.rs:133`). `ContentJump` remains a result-or-continuation category (`R/content_search.rs:392`).

- **Severity differences — retain differentiated assessments.** Ordinary logical-length spelling and expansion-notification booleans warrant low severity. Mixed coordinate spaces and directional/two-boolean operation flags warrant medium severity. These refinements do not change the established departures or authorize behavior changes.
