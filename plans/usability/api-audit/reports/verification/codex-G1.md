## My findings disputed

- **codex-C1-1 — maintain.** Claude’s C1 “no findings” disposition and preservation claim miss replacement of the value binding together with invalid reactive options. Reconciliation clears `name` and `selected` (`rt/choice_controls.rs:936–942`); validation can then return at `:1010` before restoration at `:1038–1043`. Inspection reads the cleared selection at `:1305`. SegmentedControl and InlineChoice delegate to this implementation (`:1509`, `:1647`). Existing invalid-list coverage changes the existing option signal, rather than replacing the value binding (`crates/pert-ui/tests/controls/component_bound_choices.rs:37`). The previous-presentation requirement remains explicit in `docs/contracts.md:45` and `:176–179`.

- **codex-C1-2 — maintain as a policy question only.** Claude classifies controller-owned collections and keyed content outside choice controls. The signatures remain as reported: reactive sources in `VirtualList::{new, estimated}` (`rt/virtual_list.rs:635`, `:659`) and child-adding builders in `ToolWindowLayout` (`rt/tool_windows/mod.rs:788`, `:807`). The collection exception specifies retained ownership (`docs/contracts.md:169–174`), and the tool-window contract specifies admission validation (`rt/tool_windows/mod.rs:695–699`); neither explicitly settles the constructor convention’s boundary. This requires the maintainer, not an auditor-imposed migration.

- **C2 “no findings” disposition — correct.** Accept claude-C2-1 as a consistency proposal: the API-wide `with_` uses exceed what my constructor-focused disposition considered (`rt/menu.rs:761`, `rt/drawing.rs:504`, `:1605`). Maintain that claude-C2-2 establishes no departure from the stated C2 rule.

- **codex-C3-1 — maintain all instances.** The keyed-menu qualification does not supply an existing exception. C3 explicitly puts bound state before other parameters (`rubric.md:161–163`), while `MenuItem::toggle` and `radio` put it after both key and label (`rt/menu.rs:299`, `:333`).

- **C4 classification of `TextSelection` — correct.** Withdraw my classification of its constructor as covered by documented control-limit clamping. Explicit offsets are accepted and normalized at `rt/text_selection.rs:43`; this is broader than pointer-position snapping. Accept claude-C4-1.

- **C4 treatment of `ProgressStatus` — correct.** Its enum remains composed from valid values (`rt/progress.rs:30`), but that does not dispose of the separate overshoot-clamping behavior at `:64`. Accept claude-C4-5’s policy question.

- **codex-C4-2, `ChoiceId` instance — maintain under the current rule.** The whole wrapped string is exposed through `as_str` (`core/choice.rs:50`), whereas `docs/contracts.md:1152` prescribes `get` without a string exception. Whether to add that exception remains a maintainer decision.
