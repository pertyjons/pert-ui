## My findings disputed

Codex records "no findings" for N1 and N5 and, for N2, "no additional findings in the required synonym sets" after examining binding/signal and stating that "collection selection, enabled versus active state, … successful writes versus settled notifications … describe distinct contracts". Its role table also differs from mine for `FormStatus` and `EditorSession`. Re-checked against the source:

1. **claude-N2-1 (owned versus reactive reads; `_signal` both takes and returns a signal). Maintained.** Codex's binding/signal check does not engage the evidence, which holds as stated: `status()` is owned on every receipt (`TaskReceipt::status -> TaskStatus` and the other six) but returns `ReadSignal` on `DialogController::status`, `WindowController::status` and `FieldHandle::status`; `Parameter::snapshot -> ReadSignal<ParameterSnapshot<T>>` beside the owned `AnyParameter::snapshot -> Result<AnyParameterSnapshot, _>` and `DeliveryMonitor::snapshot`; the reactive form is `*_binding` (receipts, `Runtime::command_enabled_binding`), `*_signal` (`UiSettings::zoom_signal`, `ThemeManager::{density,mode}_signal`) or `observe` (`DeliveryMonitor`); and `Runtime::command_enabled_signal(&mut self, id, enabled: impl Into<ReadSignal<bool>>)` takes the signal its sibling `command_enabled_binding` returns (semantic list, pert-ui-runtime; runtime/commands.rs:689, :1694). No contract sentence explains these.
2. **claude-N2-5, `Tabs::on_select` versus `on_selection_change`. Withdrawn.** Contracts "Component notifications" distinguishes the two: `Tabs::on_select` reports component-owned selection, while `VirtualList`, `Tree` and `Table` report changes of a bound application-owned `ListSelection`, "with both the active item and the selected keys", coalescing writes between updates (docs/contracts.md:527–534). The different names track a documented difference, so they are not two words for one concept. The other bullets of claude-N2-5 stand: `EditorSession::is_open` ("Whether an edit is open: begun and not yet ended", editor_session.rs:184–188) and `DraftEditorController::is_active` ("Whether a session … is active", text_fields/draft.rs:373–375) name the same begun-and-not-ended state of two `begin`/`end` protocols; codex's "enabled versus active" remark concerns a different pair.
3. **claude-B4-1, `FormStatus`. Corrected.** Codex classifies it as an application-owned coordinator. Its rustdoc says "Form-level coordination of field statuses, owned by the application" (field_status.rs:173), and no widget takes it (`Form::new` takes only children, form.rs:64); it reaches widgets only through the `FieldHandle`s it issues and `focus_first_error`. Whether it is in the controller role is therefore the same policy question as `ListSelection`'s application ownership (contracts.md:1160–1161), not a plain departure. What remains a departure is the suffix: `FormStatus` is a cloneable handle (`#[derive(Clone)]`, `Rc` inside, :214–216) under `*Status`, which elsewhere in the API names only owned value enums (`FieldStatus`, `DialogStatus`, `TaskStatus`, `ToastStatus`, …). B4 asks for "a suffix on a type in another role". `ContextMenuOpener`, `FieldHandle` and `ToastQueue` stand as reported.
4. **claude-B4-1's exclusion of `EditorSession`. Maintained.** See the codex-B4-1 row: it addresses no widget.
5. **claude-N5-1 (`DropPosition`, `PickStatus`). Corrected rule anchor.** Codex reports no N5 findings. C-WORD-ORDER concerns the order of words in related names; my observation is a missing family prefix, not a word order, so the guideline does not support it. I reclassify it as an N2/N5 **consistency proposal** with no guideline backing and keep the observation, which still holds: `DropPosition` (list_drag.rs:47) is used only by `ListDrop::position` and `ListDropPlacement::position` among the `ListDrop*` types, and `PickStatus` (quick_pick.rs:281) pairs with `QuickPickController` while `CommandPaletteStatus` (command_palette.rs:120) pairs with `CommandPaletteController`. Severity low, confidence medium, as before.
6. **`Pending<T>` role.** My table classifies it as a handle and codex's as a receipt. Maintained; see the codex-B4-4 row.
7. **Severity of logical-pixel `f32` lengths.** Codex rates its N3-1 medium as a whole; my claude-N3-2 rates the logical-pixel instances low. Maintained, on the ground given in the codex-N3-1 row. claude-N3-1 (physical pixels) is medium in both reports.

N1 and B4's other rows agree. claude-N2-2, claude-N2-3 and claude-N2-4 are not contradicted (codex's N2 scan names neither the permission adjectives nor the role-table accessors nor typed downcast).

### claude-N3-d1 Logical-pixel lengths taken as `Length` are reported back, and given as defaults, as bare `f32`

- **Item and rule kind:** N3, project rule (AGENTS.md "Give domain quantities … appropriate validated types"); a further set of instances of claude-N3-2, which records the same pattern for `ThemeMetrics::with_metric(Length)` against `ThemeMetrics::get -> f32`.
- **Paths and configurations:** pert-ui-runtime, all configurations (re-exported by the facade).
- **Observation (signature):**
  - `TableColumn::width(self, width: Length)` (table.rs:96) and `TableController::set_column_width(.., width: Length)` (:804) set the width, but `TableColumn::fixed_width(&self) -> Option<f32>` (:169) reads it back; `TableColumn::MIN_WIDTH: f32` (:68) bounds it;
  - `ToolWindow::extent(self, extent: Length)` (tool_windows/mod.rs:450) sets the panel extent, but the notification reports it as the public field `ToolWindowChange::extent: Option<f32>` (:236) and `ToolWindowChange::with_geometry(self, extent: Option<f32>, ..)` (:285) takes it bare; the unit is in the field's rustdoc only (:233);
  - `DRAG_THRESHOLD: f32` (drag.rs:15, "Window-logical distance the pointer travels") beside `ClickCounter::DEFAULT_DISTANCE: Length`, the analogous pointer-travel tolerance;
  - `QuickPick::DEFAULT_WIDTH: f32` (quick_pick.rs:879) and `CommandPalette::DEFAULT_WIDTH: f32` (command_palette.rs:513), the defaults of the bare `width(f32)` builders claude-N3-2 already names;
  - `Painter::text`/`rich_text`, `MeasureContext::text`/`rich_text` and `EventContext::text` take `width: Option<f32>`, while the rest of `Painter` takes `Rect`, `Point` and `Length`.
- **Rule:** N3: "A numeric parameter or return value with a unit says so, through its type … or its name." Here the same quantity changes representation between its setter and its getter, so a value read back cannot be passed to the setter without `Length::new(..)?`.
- **Severity:** low (logical pixels are the documented default space, docs/rendering-platform.md:9). **Confidence:** high for the setter/getter pairs; medium for the constants.
- **Suggested fix:** with claude-N3-2: return `Option<Length>` from `fixed_width` and in `ToolWindowChange::extent`, and type `MIN_WIDTH`, `DEFAULT_WIDTH` and `DRAG_THRESHOLD` as `Length`; or adopt claude-N3-2's convention option ("bare `f32` geometry is logical pixels") in contracts. Breaking for the type changes; `ToolWindowChange` is `#[non_exhaustive]` but its field type is public.
- **Destination:** TODO.md, with claude-N3-2.

### claude-N3-d2 `UiStrings::MAX_LENGTH` counts bytes, while `max_length` elsewhere counts grapheme clusters

- **Item and rule kind:** N3, project rule; N2, consistency proposal ("one word for two concepts").
- **Paths and configurations:** pert-ui-runtime, all.
- **Observation (signature):** `UiStrings::MAX_LENGTH: usize = 1024` is "The longest text a role accepts, in bytes" (strings.rs:576–577; its error says "longer than {max} bytes", :516). `TextField::max_length(count: Option<usize>)` and `TextArea::max_length`/`min_length` count **grapheme clusters** (text_fields.rs:450–458). The other byte limits put the unit in the name: `Workspace::MAX_STRING_BYTES`, `Workspace::MAX_PATH_BYTES`, `TextEditError::MAX_TEXT_BYTES`, `FontData::MAX_BYTES`, `RasterImage::MAX_BYTES`; character limits say `_CHARS` (`EventStatus::MAX_MESSAGE_CHARS`, `ProfileStats::MAX_KEY_CHARS`). codex-N3-2's `ResourceKind::scene_budget` is the same gap.
- **Rule:** N3 (unit through the name); N2 ("length" means bytes on one type and grapheme clusters on another).
- **Severity:** low (a translator's string of 1,024 characters in a multi-byte script is refused unexpectedly). **Confidence:** high.
- **Suggested fix:** rename to `UiStrings::MAX_BYTES` (or `MAX_TEXT_BYTES`). Breaking.
- **Destination:** TODO.md.

### claude-N2-d1 Parallel status and row vocabularies use different words for one state

- **Item and rule kind:** N2, consistency proposal (version 2's variant and field rows; version 1 had no variant names).
- **Paths and configurations:** pert-ui-runtime, all.
- **Observation (signature):**
  - nothing presented: `DialogStatus::Idle` ("No session is open", dialog.rs:24–25) but `PickStatus::Closed` and `CommandPaletteStatus::Closed` ("Nothing is presented", quick_pick.rs:285–286, command_palette.rs:122–124);
  - chosen, surface closed, awaiting the result: `PickStatus::Pending` (quick_pick.rs:290–294) but `CommandPaletteStatus::Invoking` (command_palette.rs:128–131), in two otherwise parallel pickers (`CommandPalette::DEFAULT_WIDTH = QuickPick::DEFAULT_WIDTH`, command_palette.rs:513);
  - ended successfully: `TaskStatus::Completed` ("The callback returned successfully", runtime/tasks.rs:9–10) but `ProgressStatus::Succeeded` (progress.rs:31–32) and `EventStatus::Succeeded` (diagnostics.rs:796–797);
  - availability of a picker row: `PickItem::disabled: bool` (quick_pick.rs:48) but `CommandEntry::enabled` and `CommandInfo::enabled` (runtime/commands.rs:408, :434), so a host that lists commands in a `QuickPick` writes `disabled: !entry.enabled`.
- **Rule:** N2 "two words for one concept". `Finished(Result<..>)` in `CommandStatus`/`RevealStatus`/`DialogStatus` is not counted: it carries success or failure, a different concept from `Completed`.
- **Severity:** low. **Confidence:** medium (each pair is clear in its own rustdoc; `DialogStatus::Idle` also means "no outcome awaiting acknowledgement").
- **Suggested fix:** one word per state across the surface controllers (`Closed`; `Pending` or `Invoking`) and across terminal statuses (`Succeeded` or `Completed`); `PickItem::disabled` may stay, since it matches the `disabled()` builder rule (contracts.md:1158). Breaking; AGENTS.md "do not change … callback timing or cancellation merely for spelling symmetry" limits it to names.
- **Destination:** TODO.md.

### claude-N4-d1 `NativeCloseAction::Defer` decides by `ReadSignal<Option<bool>>`

- **Item and rule kind:** N4, guideline C-CUSTOM-TYPE ("Arguments convey meaning through types, not `bool` or `Option`").
- **Paths and configurations:** `pert_ui::NativeCloseAction::Defer` (cpu, default, image-formats, images, no-default-desktop-cpu, serde, svg).
- **Observation (signature):** `Defer(crate::ReadSignal<Option<bool>>)`: "Await `Some(true)` to close or `Some(false)` to keep open" (crates/pert-ui/src/app.rs:207–210). The application decides by writing `Some(true)` or `Some(false)` into the signal from a confirmation handler, where the meaning is invisible; the packaged example needs a comment to say it ("`Some(true)` to close", crates/pert-ui/examples/diagram_editor/app.rs:891, :910–911). The same enum and `WindowCloseAction` already name the two decisions `Close` and `KeepOpen` (app.rs:194–206; pert-ui-runtime windows.rs:155–161), and round 1 had to look it up (plans/usability/runs/r1-1-codex/FRICTION.md:62–63).
- **Rule:** C-CUSTOM-TYPE; rubric N4 (the `bool` is the decision the application passes, not a builder property).
- **Severity:** low. **Confidence:** high.
- **Suggested fix:** `Defer(ReadSignal<Option<WindowCloseAction>>)`, reusing the existing `Close`/`KeepOpen` vocabulary. Breaking.
- **Destination:** TODO.md.

`EditorTrigger::Tab { reverse: bool }` (the other new `bool` payload with an operation meaning) names its field at construction, so it is not counted; it should follow claude-N4-1's direction type if that fix is adopted.
